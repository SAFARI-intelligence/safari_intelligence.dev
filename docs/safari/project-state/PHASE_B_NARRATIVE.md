# SAFARI — Phase B Narrative Architecture

- **Date:** 2026-11-03 · **Status:** SUBMITTED, not yet approved. Not implemented in the app.

## Central narrative
**KENYA. ONE COUNTRY. EVERY WORLD.** SAFARI connects people to Kenya's destinations, experiences, operators and natural heritage. Differentiator: Kenya-specific knowledge and the intelligence connecting experiences. Proverb: "Haraka haraka haina baraka".

## Three Worlds (6 stages: Introduction → Identity → Experience → Intelligence → Connection → Action)
- **The Wild** — Maasai Mara, Amboseli, Tsavo, Samburu, conservancies. Intelligence: WIS zone insights with 2-hour delay. Products: Planner, WIS, Booking.
- **The Coast** — Diani, Mombasa, Lamu, Watamu; Swahili heritage, marine life. Intelligence: seasons, tides, turtle nesting (PLANNED content). Products: Planner, Booking, Conservation.
- **The City** — Nairobi, Nairobi National Park, Karura, galleries, food, business tourism. Intelligence: airport/transfer and neighbourhood guidance (PLANNED). Products: Planner, Booking, Journal.
- **Beyond** — Mount Kenya, Rift Valley lakes, Turkana.

Note: the chat draft described City intelligence as "real-time urban navigation" — this wording violates the claims rules and must be removed before approval.

## Claims rules
- Allowed: "Built in Nairobi", 2-hour delay disclosure, KSh pricing, factual Kenyan geography.
- Label required: planner ("draft itineraries, estimated prices"), checkout ("simulated"), WIS ("sample data"), Simba Points ("preview").
- Vision only: East Africa expansion, collar/IoT feeds, carbon offsetting.
- Prohibited: "real-time"/"live" wildlife, "operating system", unverified impact counters, "guaranteed lowest rates".
- Requires evidence: any partner name, operator count, conservation outcome. The chat draft stated "In Progress" statuses for targets — see VALIDATION_TARGETS.md (UNVERIFIED).
